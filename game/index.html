ISLAND COURIER

A 3D island mail-plane run — three deliveries, eight gold rings, one tank of fuel.

Fly the little red-and-white courier plane across a tropical archipelago. Thread every glowing ring, then put her down gently on the island strip to deliver the mail. Landings refuel the plane; hard landings bend it. The clock charges by the second, so fly clean lines — not just full throttle.

W / S or ↑ / ↓ — pitch up / down
A / D or ← / → — bank and turn
SHIFT / CTRL (or + / −) — throttle up / down
Mouse drag — look around  ·  double-click — reset view
C / V — cockpit view / chase view

Watch the gold arrow: it points at your next ring or runway. Keep the needle out of the red — below 16 kt the wing stalls.

PAUSED

The courier is holding overhead. Take a breath, check the chart, then get back to the mail run.

CRASHED

The mail is late, but the pilot walked away. Fuel, damage and the clock are what decide a sortie — fly the next one cleaner.

Tip: line up with the strip early, keep speed under 95 kt, and flare — sink gently under 6 m/s.
